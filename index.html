Research · Biomedical Engineering · Carnegie Mellon University

Temporary liver support for ammonia removal

My M.S. research investigated a temporary liver-support concept for removing ammonia in acute liver failure. I developed and experimentally evaluated a prototype system using a PDMS membrane, perfluorocarbon, and vacuum pressure in a simulated peritoneal environment.

The work combined prototype development, laboratory experimentation, fluid handling, instrumentation, calibration, troubleshooting, MATLAB, statistical analysis, and technical communication.

Biomedical research grounded in physical experimentation. Prototype development · Sensors · Experimental testing · MATLAB · Data analysis

Project · Mechanical Design + Test · Purdue University

Rocketcam pneumatic air cannon

As team lead on a senior mechanical design capstone, I helped redesign Purdue's pneumatic launch system into a smaller, portable, rapid-fire device capable of launching a 3D-printed rocket carrying a GoPro camera.

I designed and built a pressure-test system, integrated a pressure transducer with an Arduino microcontroller, wrote custom C++ for time-resolved pressure data collection, and used experimental results to compare trigger mechanisms and guide design decisions.

Project · Reverse Engineering · Purdue University

FDM printer cartridge reverse engineering

As a laboratory assistant, I reverse engineered the cartridge system of a commercial Stratasys Dimension SST 768 FDM printer so its non-refillable cartridges could be reused with lower-cost replacement filament.

The project crossed mechanical engineering, electronics, programming, Linux, and EEPROM analysis. The resulting process reduced consumable material cost by about 86%, using roughly $28 replacement filament in place of about $200 proprietary filament while reusing the cartridge.

About 86% lower consumable material cost. ~$200 proprietary filament · ~$28 replacement filament · Cartridge reuse · EEPROM reset · Linux

Tool · Browser Software

SOPTE — Simple Offline Plain Text Editor

SOPTE is a browser-based plain text editor I developed for working directly with local files while keeping the interface simple and the application usable offline.

It grew out of practical needs rather than a software exercise: reliable editing, recovery snapshots, and responsive handling of large documents without turning a plain text editor into a complicated application.

A usable tool, not just a software case study. Offline-first · Local files · Recovery · Large-document editing

Research · Software + Engineering Education · Purdue University

CADCompare

I was lead software developer and co-author on published research for CADCompare, a web application designed to compare PDF CAD drawings and highlight differences that might otherwise be missed during manual review.

The work brought together engineering graphics, software development, user testing, and research communication, and was published through the American Society for Engineering Education.

Art · Permanent Public Art · Jeffersonville, Indiana

Blocks of Love

Created with Jennie DiBeneditto for Clark Memorial Hospital, Blocks of Love combines 144 individually cut plywood blocks, algorithmically randomized block heights, laser-printed imagery, kiln-fired vitreous enamel, acrylic, and clear coat.

The work represents another part of the same hands-on practice: materials, fabrication, process planning, visual design, and making physical objects intended to communicate with people outside the studio or laboratory.
